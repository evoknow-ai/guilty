PRIVACY.

Your browsing data stays on your device.

Guilty records the domains you actively use and the time spent on them so it can create your private activity reports.

This information is stored only in Chrome's local extension storage. Guilty has no server, analytics, advertising, account system, or remote data transmission.

You can pause tracking, exclude uncategorized websites, erase tracking history, and export or restore settings from the Settings page.
