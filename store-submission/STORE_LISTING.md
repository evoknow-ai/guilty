## Detailed description

Guilty shows where your active browsing time goes without sending your browsing history anywhere.

Unlike basic screen-time tools, Guilty lets you organize websites into categories that match your life. Create categories such as Work, AI, Social Media, Shopping, Research, or anything else. Use exact domains or wildcard rules, then see clear reports for today, this week, last week, this month, and this year.

KEY FEATURES

• Tracks active browsing time, not merely open tabs
• Custom categories and wildcard domain rules
• Inline assignment of uncategorized domains
• This Week versus Last Week comparison bars
• Configurable colors for every category
• Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly reports
• Complete shareable PNG reports
• Settings backup and restore
• Adjustable idle timeout
• Pause tracking or erase history at any time

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Guilty has no account, backend, analytics, or advertising. Website domains, activity time, categories, rules, colors, and preferences remain in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. Guilty does not read page content and does not transmit browsing activity to the developer or third parties.

Guilty requests access to websites solely to identify the active domain and detect local interaction signals needed to measure active use. Clipboard access is used only after you click Share PNG.

Your attention, accounted for.

## Single purpose

Guilty measures active time spent on website domains and organizes that time into user-configurable categories for private, on-device browsing activity reports.

## Permission justifications

### storage

Required to save activity history, domain-category rules, report colors, and user preferences in Chrome's local extension storage. This data never leaves the user's device.

### host permission: all websites

Required because Guilty's core purpose is to measure active browsing time across websites chosen by the user. A lightweight content script detects only the current domain, page visibility, focus, and recent user interaction. It does not read page content, form data, passwords, or messages.

### clipboardWrite

Used only when the user clicks Share PNG to copy the user-generated activity report image to the clipboard. Guilty never reads the clipboard.
